Frequently asked questions

Are my PDFs uploaded to a server?

No. Every tool on this page runs in your browser, so your files never leave your device.

How do I compress a PDF to under 100 KB or 200 KB?

Open the Compress tab, choose your PDF, pick “Target size” and select 100 KB, 200 KB or a custom size. The tool tries several quality levels and keeps the sharpest one that fits. Very long documents may not reach a very small target; you will be told the smallest size that is still readable.

Does compression change my document?

Yes. Pages are saved as images, so text can no longer be selected, searched or copied, and fillable form fields are flattened. Merge, split, reorder and rotate do not change the content of your pages at all.

How do I merge PDF files?

On the Organize tab, add all the PDFs, drag the pages into the order you want and choose “Download PDF”.

Can I use password-protected PDFs?

No. Remove the password first, then add the file.

Is there a file size or page limit?

There is no fixed limit, but everything runs on your device, so very large files can be slow on phones and older computers.
