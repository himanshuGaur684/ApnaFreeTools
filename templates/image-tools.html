Frequently asked questions

How do I resize an image to 20 KB or 50 KB?

On the Compress & Resize tab, add your image, choose 20 KB or 50 KB under “File size” and press Compress. The tool lowers the quality first and, only if needed, makes the picture slightly smaller in pixels until it fits.

How do I make a photo and signature for an exam or government form?

Open the Photo & Signature tab, pick a preset or type the width, height and KB limits from your form, add your image and drag the box to frame it. The result has exactly the pixel size you asked for and stays within the file-size limits.

Are the preset sizes official?

They are sizes that forms commonly ask for, but requirements differ between organisations and change over time. Always check the instructions on your form; every number can be edited.

My form needs a minimum file size. What if my image is too small?

Enter the minimum in “Min KB”. If the finished image is below it, the file is padded with unused data to reach the minimum. The picture itself is not changed.

Are my images uploaded?

No. Everything runs in your browser, so your photos and signature never leave your device.

Which format should I choose?

JPG for photos and almost all online forms, PNG for graphics, screenshots and transparent backgrounds, and WebP for the smallest files on websites.
