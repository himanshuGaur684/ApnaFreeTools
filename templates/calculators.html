Frequently asked questions

How is GST calculated?

To add GST: GST = price × rate ÷ 100, and total = price + GST. To find the GST inside a total: price = total ÷ (1 + rate ÷ 100). Within a state the GST is split equally into CGST and SGST; between states it is charged as IGST.

How is EMI calculated?

EMI = P × r × (1 + r)ⁿ ÷ ((1 + r)ⁿ − 1), where P is the loan amount, r is the monthly interest rate (yearly rate ÷ 12 ÷ 100) and n is the number of months.

How does the SIP calculator work?

Each monthly instalment is assumed to grow at the yearly return divided by twelve, compounded monthly. With a step-up, the instalment rises by that percentage every year. Real returns move up and down, so treat the result as an illustration.

Which income tax rates does the calculator use?

The slabs announced in Budget 2025, which Budget 2026 left unchanged. New regime: nil up to ₹4 lakh, then 5%, 10%, 15%, 20% and 25% on each further ₹4 lakh, and 30% above ₹24 lakh, with a ₹75,000 standard deduction for salaried people and no tax on taxable income up to ₹12 lakh. Old regime: nil up to ₹2.5 lakh (₹3 lakh from age 60, ₹5 lakh from age 80), then 5%, 20% and 30%, with a ₹50,000 standard deduction. A 4% cess and any surcharge are added.

Is the income tax result exact?

It is an estimate for ordinary income such as salary, pension and interest. It does not handle capital gains, other special-rate income or every deduction, so check with the Income Tax Department’s calculator or a tax professional before filing.

How do I convert CGPA to percentage?

CBSE uses CGPA × 9.5. Many universities use CGPA × 10 or (CGPA − 0.75) × 10, and some have their own formula. Always use the formula your board or university publishes.
